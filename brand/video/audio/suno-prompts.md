# Suno prompts: the bleh.xyz theme

Three directions for the 30 second film theme, an optional chant, and the 5 second
sting. You run these in Suno; the mixer does the rest. Nothing here names an artist
(Suno rejects that, and the brand does not borrow anyone's sound).

## What the music has to do

| Film time | Bars at 106 BPM | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00–1.25 | 2-beat pickup | Wordmark draws on | Almost nothing. A hum, a count-in click. The logo draw is a sound effect |
| 1.25–3.51 | bar 1 | **Tongue pops (1.25)**, tagline | One hit on the pop, then let it ring. Sparse |
| 3.51–9.17 | bars 2–4 (first 2 beats of 4) | The problem | Deadpan. Bass and hats only, muted, a bit bored |
| 9.17–10.31 | last 2 beats of bar 4 | Tongue wipe | Tape-stop or a hard break. Silence is fine |
| 10.31–12.57 | bar 5 | **The bleh drop (10.35)** | The hook, alone, stop-time: two notes falling a minor third ("bleh") |
| 12.57–20.50 | bars 6–9 | What you get | Full groove comes in. Confident, unhurried |
| 20.50–26.16 | bars 9–11 | How it stays up | Same groove, one new bright layer. At 24.95 a mirror is taken down and **the music must not react** (that is the joke) |
| 26.16–27.29 | first 2 beats of bar 12 | Tongue wipe | Break again |
| 27.29–28.42 | last 2 beats of bar 12 | **End-card pop (27.2)** | The hook once more |
| 28.42–30.00 | bar 13 | End card holds | Button ending: one final hit on the downbeat, rings out, **silent by 30.0** |

Why 106 BPM: with a two-beat pickup, beats land within 0.1 s of the pop at 1.25, the
scene change at 3.5, the wipe at 9.2, the drop at 10.35, the scene change at 12.5, the
wipe at 26.2 and the end-card pop at 27.2, and four of those are bar downbeats. A bar is
2.264 s and a beat 0.566 s. Half or double time (53 / 212) fits the same grid.

Why E minor / G major: the pitched sound effects are in it. The "shrug" effect and the
placeholder hook are G falling to E, and the end-card button is an E chord. A theme in
another key will still work, but the button hit may rub against the last chord.

## How to use Suno for this

1. Open **Create**, switch on **Custom**.
2. Paste one **Style of Music** text below into the style field.
3. Instrumental directions: tick **Instrumental**. If your takes ignore the structure,
   untick it and paste the "structure tags" block into the Lyrics field instead. Tags in
   square brackets are stage directions, not sung words.
4. Set a title (anything; `bleh theme A` is fine) and generate. **Make at least six
   takes per direction.** Suno gives two per click.
5. Suno does not follow second-by-second timing and will hand you one to three minutes,
   not 30 seconds. That is expected. You are listening for a take that contains, in
   order: a sparse opening, a break, a clear two-note hook moment, a groove, and a clean
   final hit. Its tempo must really be 106 (tap along, or check in any tempo app).
6. Shape the take:
   - **Crop** (Edit → Crop, paid plans) to keep the 30 s you want, or just note the
     timestamp where the hook drop happens and let the mixer line it up (see handoff).
   - If the take has no ending, use **Extend** from about 0:24 with the lyrics field set
     to `[Outro]` `[Final hit]` `[End]`, and pick the extension that stops cleanly.
   - If the opening is too busy, use **Replace Section** on the first few seconds with
     `[Intro] [Sparse] [Single hit]`.
7. If nothing lands on the grid, generate with the BPM stated twice in the style text
   ("106 BPM, exactly 106 beats per minute") or try the half-time wording (53 BPM feel).

## Direction A: shrug garage (recommended starting point)

Lo-fi garage with a bassline that sounds like it could not care less.

**Style of Music**

```
lo-fi garage punk instrumental, 106 BPM, E minor, deadpan shrugging fuzz bassline, dry tight drums, muted palm-mute guitar stabs, one cheap combo organ note, two-note falling hook, dry room sound, cheeky, nonchalant, precise, short button ending
```

**Instrumental:** ticked.

**Structure tags** (only if you untick Instrumental):

```
[Intro: room hum, stick click, no drums]
[Hit: one fuzz bass note, let it ring]
[Verse: bass and hi-hat only, muted, deadpan]
[Break: tape stop, silence]
[Drop: two-note falling hook alone, stop-time]
[Chorus: full band groove, fuzz bass, dry drums, guitar stabs]
[Chorus: same groove, add organ]
[Break]
[Hook: two-note falling hook]
[Final hit]
[End]
```

## Optional vocal variant: the deadpan chant

One word, used three times. Works over any direction: untick **Instrumental**, keep the
same style text and add `, deadpan spoken gang chant, no singing, no other vocals` to
the end of it. Paste this into Lyrics:

```
[Intro: instrumental, no vocals]

[Verse: instrumental, no vocals]

[Break]

[Drop: deadpan spoken, one voice, dry]
Bleh.

[Chorus: instrumental groove, gang chant on the last beat of every second bar]
(bleh)
(bleh)

[Break]

[Outro: deadpan spoken]
Bleh.

[Final hit]
[End]
```

Keep it only if the voice is flat and bored. If Suno sings it, adds ad-libs, or adds any
other word, bin the take. The film's on-screen text is the script; the voice must not
compete with it. If the spoken "Bleh." lands on the drop, lower the tongue-pop effect at
10.35 by 3 dB in the cue sheets so the two do not fight.

## The 5 second sting

**Default: do nothing.** With no separate sting file, the mixer cuts the sting's music
from the end of the theme (from 26.04 s), so the theme's last hook and final hit play
under the logo: hook note on the tongue pop at 1.25 s, final hit near the tagline at
2.4 s, ring-out, done. One theme, one sound, and the sting always matches the film.

If you want a dedicated jingle, Suno cannot make five seconds, so generate a short piece
and crop it:

**Style of Music** (use the instrument words from whichever direction you chose)

```
5 second logo sting, audio logo jingle, 106 BPM, E minor, one beat of silence, single punchy hit, two-note falling hook, one final hit, ring out, very short, no intro, no build
```

**Instrumental:** ticked. Generate many takes, **Crop** to the best five seconds so the
first hit sits 1.25 s after the start, and export it as `sting.wav`.

## Export and handoff

1. In Suno: the take's **⋯ menu → Download → WAV Audio** (paid plans). If you only have
   MP3, download that; the mixer reads either.
2. Save the file as:

   ```
   brand/video/audio/music/theme.wav
   brand/video/audio/music/sting.wav     (only if you made a separate sting)
   ```

   (For an MP3 keep the real extension and pass it with `--music`, see below.)
3. Find where the hook drop happens in your file, in seconds, by ear in any player.
   In the film the drop is the downbeat at **10.31 s**. So:

   ```
   music offset = (drop time in your file) − 10.31
   ```

   Drop at 10.3 in the file: offset 0, nothing to pass. Drop at 24.8: offset 14.49.
   Drop at 8.0 (the track starts too late for the film): offset −2.31, and the mixer
   holds the music back by that much.
4. Run the mixer from the repository root:

   ```sh
   node brand/video/audio/mix.mjs                                  # theme.wav cropped and aligned in Suno already
   node brand/video/audio/mix.mjs --music-offset 14.49             # theme.wav, drop found at 24.8 s
   node brand/video/audio/mix.mjs --music ~/Downloads/take3.mp3 --music-offset 14.49
   node brand/video/audio/mix.mjs --sting-music brand/video/audio/music/sting.wav
   ```

5. Watch `brand/video/bleh-30s-1920x1080-sound.mp4`. Check three moments: the pop at
   1.25, the drop at 10.35, the end at 28.4–30.0. If the music does not end by itself
   before 30.0, add `--fade-out 1.5`: the end-card button effect covers a fade well.
6. Licence check before publishing: music made on Suno's free plan is not cleared for
   commercial use. Generate the final take on a paid plan and keep a note of the plan
   and date next to the file. Read Suno's current terms yourself; this is not legal advice.
