Terms of use

Plain terms for an open project. The canonical text is docs/terms.md in the repository; this page carries the same words.

In short

  • The project's own data is CC0 1.0 and its code is Apache-2.0. Use them for anything.
  • Some values come from publishers that attach a notice. Keep that notice with the values whenever you pass them on.
  • Everything is provided as is. It may be late, wrong, incomplete or unavailable. It is not investment advice.
  • bleh.xyz is not affiliated with any source it reads.

01What these terms cover

These terms cover the bleh.xyz website and the data the project publishes from servers it operates, today https://data.bleh.xyz/.

bleh.xyz is an early preview. The only data service that is live is the web snapshot at https://data.bleh.xyz/web/v0/, described in web snapshot v0. The live gateway, relays and archive described elsewhere in the repository are not open to the public yet. These terms will cover them when they are.

Copies served by other people, such as mirrors, are outside the project's control. These terms still describe the license of the data in them.

You may copy, change, redistribute and sell it without asking and without credit.

The licensing decision is ADR 0002. CC0 covers only rights the project holds. It does not override a source's own conditions, which the next section explains.

03Source notices travel with the data

Every value comes from a source admitted under the admission policy, and every record names its admission ID. Some sources let anyone copy and redistribute their data on conditions: a notice or disclaimer that travels with the data, credit, no implied endorsement, and labelling of changed values. For those records the project's CC0 dedication still applies to its own contribution, and the source's conditions pass through to you (ADR 0012, decision 5).

Where the notices are. Every published file carries the notices for the records in it:

  • in the web snapshot, the notice field of each entry in latest.json sources[] and of every per-source file;
  • in a records archive, _notices.json at the archive root and the notices list of every signed manifest (manifest v0), once archives are published.

What you must do. When you show, republish or redistribute values, keep each source's notice with the values it covers. Do not suggest that a source endorses you or your product. Label values you have changed, and do not present changed values as the source's own.

The main example is the Federal Reserve Bank of New York's reference rates (ADM-0007). Their notice says the rates are subject to the New York Fed's Terms of Use, that the New York Fed is not responsible for their republication and has no liability for your use, and that bleh.xyz is not affiliated with or endorsed by it. For SOFR and BGCR, and rates derived from them, the notice also carries the disclaimer the New York Fed requires for data it uses under license from DTCC Solutions LLC. Copy the notice as published; do not paraphrase it.

Some other notices are a credit line or a condition that values presented as the publisher's own stay unaltered. The simple rule covers all of them: keep every notice with its values.

05Availability

There is no service level agreement and no guaranteed availability.

  • Today: the web snapshot is an early preview with no availability target. It is normally refreshed every few minutes, and it may be stale, change or be unreachable at any time. latest.json carries generated_ms and each source's last_record_ms, so you can check freshness yourself.
  • Published target, once the live gateway is in production: at least 99.9% per 30 days, measured by an external probe that connects once a minute, subscribes to one instrument and must receive a block heartbeat within 5 seconds, with announced node upgrades excluded and listed on the status page (requirements). This is a target, not a promise. The measured figure will be published as measured.

Field names in a versioned path such as v0 are not removed or renamed in place; such a change gets a new path (v1 beside v0, ADR 0016).

No key or sign-up is needed. Fetch latest.json and then only the files whose sha256 has changed. Traffic that degrades the service for others may be limited.

06Reporting problems

No dedicated reporting address is set yet. Until one is, use the repository on GitHub, codeurge/bleh.xyz:

  • Wrong values, broken files, bugs and questions: open a GitHub issue.
  • Security problems: vulnerabilities, signing-key compromise, forged observations, and contamination (any sign that an output contains exchange-licensed data or data derived from it). Report these privately through GitHub private vulnerability reporting on the repository, never in a public issue. SECURITY.md describes what happens next.
  • A publisher's concerns: a publisher who believes the project carries its data against its terms can report it the same private way. Affected outputs are withdrawn under the admission policy's Removal rule.
  • Conduct: the code of conduct applies to all project spaces. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers; reports are handled confidentially.

When a reporting address is set, it will be published in SECURITY.md, the code of conduct and these terms.

These terms may change. Each change gets a new version number and date here and an entry in CHANGELOG.md; earlier versions stay in the repository's history. A change cannot take back the CC0 dedication or the Apache-2.0 license of anything already published under them.
