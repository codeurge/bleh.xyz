A price is a fact. Facts are free.

Seven short claims about who should be allowed to look at a market. We are building bleh.xyz to make them true. It is not finished.

  1. Prices are discovered in public.

    Every trade teaches the whole market something. The lesson was paid for by the people who traded, not by whoever printed the receipt.

  2. A contract is not a law of nature.

    Licensed feeds bind whoever touches them, and whatever is derived from them. So we will not touch them. Not once, not upstream, not "just to check".

  3. Measure the same thing from open ground.

    The same economic exposure now trades where every order is public. We read those markets from our own nodes, add the swap trades the law already publishes, and say exactly what each number is.

  4. Call a proxy a proxy.

    These are not exchange prices and we will never dress them up as such. Thin markets are labelled as tapes. Gaps are published as gaps. The basis gets measured, not hidden.

  5. Do not trust us. Check.

    Every block is signed. Every method is versioned. Every output can be recomputed from the archive by someone who thinks we are wrong.

  6. Make every copy the original.

    Public domain data, content-addressed files, signed manifests anchored on Bitcoin, mirrors on networks nobody runs. If we vanish, the data should not notice.

  7. Nothing to sell.

    No tiers, no API keys, no token, no sales call. Code under Apache-2.0, data under CC0. Take it, and do not ask.

That is the whole position. To the order forms, the embargoes and the audits, our considered response is one syllable long.
